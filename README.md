Led the development of a blockchain-based system that tokenised shares on the Nairobi Securities Exchange, compressing trade settlement from **T+3 days → near-instant**. Selected for the **NSE Incubator Programme** after winning **1st Runner-Up at the UoN/NSE Hedera Hackathon**.

`Hedera` `TypeScript` `Smart Contracts` `Fintech` `Financial Infrastructure`

### 🌍 [OrionRamp](https://orionramp.com)
> *Crypto on/off-ramp infrastructure for African DApps*

Co-founded and architected a full-stack crypto fiat gateway that enables DApps across Africa to offer seamless on-ramp and off-ramp services. Built on Hedera with deep integrations across local payment rails.

`Hedera` `Node.js` `React` `Fintech` `DeFi` `Africa`

### 🧠 AI Underwriting Automation — Kenya Reinsurance Corporation

Designed and deployed an enterprise-grade AI automation platform for facultative underwriting at Kenya Re. Reduced worksheet generation time from **multiple days → minutes**, and implemented AI-driven professional indemnity analysis — while winning **3rd Place at the Kenya Re AI Hackathon**.

`Python` `FastAPI` `AI Agents` `LLM` `Insurance` `Automation`
